Chapter 1

Introduction to AI Engineering

Foundation models flipped the old ML workflow on its head: instead of training a model for your product, you now start from a powerful general-purpose model and adapt it. AI engineering is the discipline of building applications on top of these models.

From ML engineering to AI engineering

The biggest shift: model development used to come first, now it comes last — if at all.

The AI engineering stack

Three layers of responsibility. Most application builders live in the top layer and reach into the lower two only when needed.

The adaptation ladder

When building with a foundation model, climb this ladder from the bottom. Each step up buys more control — and costs much more effort.

Key takeaways

  • Start from capability AI engineering builds on pretrained foundation models — model development is now the last resort, not the first step.
  • Adaptation is the skill Prompting, RAG, and finetuning are the core techniques. Learn when each one is the right tool.
  • Evaluation is the bottleneck Demos are easy; proving quality is hard. Evaluation skill separates toy projects from products.
  • Climb the ladder bottom-up Try the cheapest technique first. Only escalate when you've measured that the cheaper one falls short.
