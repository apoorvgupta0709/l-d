Chapter 4

Evaluating AI Systems

Chapter 3 gave you scoring methods; this chapter is about using them on your system: deciding what to measure, picking the right model from hundreds of options, and building an evaluation pipeline you can trust release after release.

What to measure — the criteria

"Is it good?" breaks down into four families of criteria. Define yours before you look at a single leaderboard.

The model selection funnel

Hundreds of models, four filters. Cheap, broad filters first; your own data last.

Your evaluation pipeline

Evaluation isn't a one-off report — it's a pipeline that runs every time your system changes.

Key takeaways

  • Criteria before models Decide what "good" means for your product before comparing models — otherwise leaderboards decide for you.
  • Public benchmarks shortlist, your evals decide Contamination and task mismatch make leaderboards a filter, never a verdict.
  • Small, real eval sets win 100 examples from your actual users beat 10,000 generic ones. Grow the set from production failures.
  • Evaluate continuously Every prompt tweak or model swap can regress something. Make evals as routine as running tests.
