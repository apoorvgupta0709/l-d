Key takeaways

  • Finetune last Exhaust prompting (cheap) and consider RAG (for knowledge) first — with evals proving the gap each time.
  • Facts vs form RAG fixes what the model knows; finetuning fixes how it behaves. Misdiagnosing this wastes months.
  • LoRA changed the economics Training under 1% of parameters puts finetuning on a single GPU — and swappable adapters enable per-customer models.
  • Distillation is the killer app Finetuning a small model on a big model's outputs is how you cut serving costs 10× without losing your task's quality.
