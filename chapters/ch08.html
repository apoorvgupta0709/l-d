Chapter 8

Dataset Engineering

Whether you're finetuning, evaluating, or distilling, the ceiling on your results is your data. Dataset engineering is the unglamorous craft that decides model quality — three properties to balance, a pipeline to build, and a synthesis shortcut with sharp edges.

The three pillars of a good dataset

Model performance rests on all three. Teams obsess over quantity; the other two matter more.

The data curation pipeline

Raw data becomes training data through a pipeline — every stage exists because skipping it once burned somebody.

Synthetic data — the shortcut with sharp edges

When real examples are scarce, models can manufacture them. Powerful — with two rules: verify, and check the license.

Key takeaways

  • Data is the ceiling No training trick rescues bad data. A few thousand excellent examples beat mountains of mediocre ones.
  • Quality, coverage, quantity — in that order Correct and consistent first, representative second, big third.
  • Dedupe before you split Duplicates leaking across train/test silently inflate every eval you run.
  • Synthetic data must be verified Generation is easy; verification (execution, judges, human sampling) is what makes it usable.
