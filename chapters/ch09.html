Chapter 9

Inference Optimization

A model that's too slow or too expensive doesn't ship. Optimization starts with measuring the right latencies — then pulling levers at the model level, the service level, or both.

The anatomy of a request

"Latency" is two different numbers, produced by two different phases. Users feel each one differently.

The optimization map

Two places to attack: change the model, or serve it smarter. The big wins in each are here.

Key takeaways

  • Measure TTFT and TPOT separately First-token wait and streaming pace have different causes and different fixes.
  • Output length is a latency lever Total time ≈ TTFT + TPOT × tokens. Concise outputs are the cheapest speedup available.
  • Quantize first, distill for scale Quantization is cheap and usually safe; distillation is the big-ticket win for narrow, high-volume tasks.
  • Serving tricks are quality-free wins Batching, prompt caching, and speculative decoding change cost and speed without changing outputs.
